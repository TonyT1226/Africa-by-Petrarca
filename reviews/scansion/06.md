# Book VI — scansion review and delivery tags

`scan/manual/06.tsv` corrects **68 of 916 verses**. `python3 tools/check_manual.py 06` prints `06 OK`.
I read all 916 rows against the Latin. I then ran two machine cross-checks over the feet. The first listed every syllable placed in a short slot of a dactyl. The second listed every open syllable placed as the second half of a spondee. I reviewed both lists by hand.

The scanner has no lexicon of natural vowel lengths, so most errors have one cause. Where several patterns fit, it gave a long vowel a short slot, or a short vowel a long one. This happened in rows marked `ok` as well as in `amb` rows.

## Uncertain or left as is

- **197** *Maior inopina mox et generosior ales* has no fully classical scansion. I chose `Máior i|nópi|ná — mox |ét gene|rósior |áles`. It needs the *no* of *inŏpīna* lengthened, plus *-ā* at the caesura (or the ablative *inopinā … parte poli*). The scanner's version needed four violations.
- These were kept as the scanner had them, because no licence-free scansion exists:
  - 31 *dĕtur*
  - 149 *Massīnissa*
  - 227 *Sīdŏnio*
  - 287 *frŭgibus*
  - 389 *Brŭtiorum*
  - 410 *lĕnis*
  - 440 *inhŏneste* with *ho* lengthened
  - 452 *Crōto*
  - 497 *decōra*
  - 548 *Trasimeni memorem*: two licences either way; I kept the version whose accents follow natural word stress
  - 626 *-que* lengthened
  - 734 *Lĭvius*
  - 801 spondaic 5th foot *conspecta*
  - 870 *Etrŭria*
  - *Sardinia*: long *dī* in 239, short *dĭ* in 685
- Petrarch's habitual prosody was accepted:
  - *Lĕlius* (consistent across Books III–VIII)
  - *Scĭpio* in mid-verse (288, 441, 818)
  - monosyllabic *dii/diis* and *cui*
- **837** *Ianue*: the metre needs either synizesis or a short *Ia-*. I wrote vocalic *Ianué* so that the Italian voice does not read "Ianvé".
- Some dashes after a proclitic *et* at the penthemimeral were left alone, because no usable hephthemimeral exists: 49, 118, 271, 412, 447, 491, 530, 659, 664, 763. They are metrically legitimate but sound slightly awkward.
- Bar-only quirks that do not change the TTS text were left: 47 *males|uádus* (the checker cannot accept "suádus"), 914 *ab|iéns*, and several bars placed after an onset consonant.
