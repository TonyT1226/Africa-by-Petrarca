# Book IV: scansion check and delivery tags

I checked all 388 verses by hand against natural quantities, not only the `var`/`amb`/`irr`/`frag` rows.
`scan/manual/04.tsv` has 36 lines:
- 33 are corrections.
- 1 is NOSCAN (299).
- 2 add foot marks to lacuna lines whose text is already right (45, 388).

`python3 tools/check_manual.py 04` prints `04 OK`.

## Corrections (33 verses)

**A. Initial vocalic V read as a consonant (12):** 9, 31, 49, 85, 97, 137, 153, 180, 208, 259, 260, 287.
The scanner turned *Vnum, Vt, Vrbs …* into a consonant cluster, so the word lost its first syllable. Every line in
the book that starts with a vocalic V was mis-scanned this way, and the text read `Vnúm`, `Vtqué`, `Vrbís`. These
lines now read `Únum`, `Útque`, `Úrbis` and so on. Several patterns change completely:
- 9: SSSSDX becomes SSSDDX.
- 31: SDSDDX. *roseam* no longer needs synizesis.
- 153: DDSSDX with *leŏ*, not synizesis of *leo*.
- 287: DSDSDX. *dies* is now regular.

**B. Impossible natural quantities (7):**
- 6: *iuvenis* has a short ŭ and *terrae* ends in a long ē. The scan is now `áut iuve|nís — ter|ré de`.
- 84: *strāvit* is long and *iaculis* has a consonantal i. Now `áut stra|vít — iacu|lís`.
- 155: *lacessītus*, then *iaculis* with a consonantal i.
- 185: *caelī* is long. Now DSDDDX, the scanner's second candidate.
- 271: *piceusque* has three vowels, but `-eusque` was taken as a diphthong. Now `pice|úsque — vo|láns replet`.
- 275: *Fāmae* is long both ways. Now `Húc Fa|mé sig|nántis — i|tér`.
- 312: *tēmōne* has a long ē. Now SSDDDX, the scanner's second candidate.

**C. u/v (1):** 22. *precipve* is wrong and now reads *precipue*: `précipu|é`.

**D. Elision before a consonantal i (4):** 30 *solitoque iocosior*, 176 *tellure iacentem*, 209 *talique iacentes*,
326 *sacra Iovem*. The vowel no longer elides there; the pattern is unchanged except at 326.

**E. Caesura dash only (8):**
- Dash after a proclitic monosyllable, moved or dropped:
  - 26 *At —* is dropped; the comma after *nunc* serves.
  - 74 *cum —* moves to after *noceat*.
  - 133 *et —* moves to after *medias*.
  - 270 *ceu cum —* is dropped; the semicolon after *petens* serves.
  - 352 *fit de —* moves to after *meritis*.
  - 366 *quam cum —* moves to after *validos*.
  - 153 also had *in — pratis*; its dash now follows *pratis* (counted under A).
- Penthemimeral after a prodelided *est*: the scanner cannot see it, so it put the dash in the wrong place.
  - 77 now reads *ascensu'st — crescens*.
  - 376 now reads *audita'st — gemitu*.
  - 85 already has a comma there, so its dash is dropped (counted under A).

**F. Stray diacritic (1):** 92. `Dî́s` (circumflex plus acute) becomes `Dís`.

## Other rows
- **299 NOSCAN.** The word `<aiment>` is corrupt, and the tts would have read the angle brackets aloud. I replaced the

  someone adopts a conjecture such as *tument*, the line scans `Iámque tu|mént am|nés, iam |quí modo |ségnis a|bíbat,`.

## Scanner bugs that probably affect other books
- `norm_word` does not map an initial vocalic V to u.
- The `NO_INIT_J` prefixes `io` and `iac` make *Iovem, iocosus, iacere, iaculum* vowel-initial. That causes the wrong
  elisions above and the `var` notes on *Iouis* and *iaculis*.
- `-eusque` is treated as a diphthong.
- The caesura after a prodelided *est* is not detected.
- The scanner has no lexicon of natural quantities, so a line marked `ok` can still be wrong (6, 185, 275, 312).
