# Book I: scansion check and delivery tags

I read all 594 verses against the Latin. The 9 `amb` and 5 `var` rows got special attention. I corrected **30 verses** in `scan/manual/01.tsv`. `python3 tools/check_manual.py 01` prints `01 OK`.

## Corrections (30)

### A. Wrong quantities or wrong foot pattern (7)
- **64** *Romana*: Rō- is long, so the line is SDDSDX (`Ét Ro|mán'ite|rúm`), not a dactyl `Ét Roman'`.
- **130** *puluere* is *pulvere*: the u is consonantal (the scanner had 4 syllables). *aestīvo* has a long ī. The line is DSSSDX: `Fébus et |ésti|vó — de|térgit |púlvere |cúrrum.` The old version put a spondee in the 5th foot on a short e.
- **148** *Itala* goes with *fronte*, so it is ablative *Ĭtalā*. The old version put ictus on the short *ta*, and *dē-* fell short. The line is SDSSDX with short Ĭ, as Petrarch also has in 2 and 184.
- **347** *efflāvēre*: flā is long. The line is SDSDDX (`Éffla|vér'ani|mós`).
- **406** *vīcīnā (ab arbore)*: cī is long. The line is SDSDDX (`Víci|náque tre|méns`).
- **444** *haudquāquam*: quā is long. The line is DSDSDX (`háudquaqu'|hóc homi|ní — For|túna`). I put the dash after *homini*, not after the weak *qu'hóc*.
- **588** *Vībrăt ĕt Ēōā*. The scanner had a long *brat* and a short Ē. The line is DSSSDX: `Víbrat et |Éo|á — iam …`.

### B. Initial consonantal i treated as a vowel, giving a false elision (4)
- **275** *Inuisam iaculis* is not elided. Pattern SDDSDX: `Ínvi|sám iacu|lís — …`. The old version put a dactyl on *invīs-*.
- **337** `núbe ia|cétis`, **353** `súmque Io|vém`, **453** `símque ia|céntes`: no elision. The pattern does not change; the e of *nube* and the *que* are restored.

### C. Incomplete verses (2)
- **357** and **361** are unfinished or lacunose lines (see the TEXTNOTES). The scanner had forced 6 feet onto them: SSSSSX, and for 361 "relax". Both rows are not `frag`, and the checker demands 6 feet for those, so both are now `NOSCAN` plain text.
- The partial scansions, if anyone later sets these rows to `frag`:
  - 357: `Ét mo|rés habi|túsque vi|rúm gres|súmque;`
  - 361: `Púnica.` [gap] `…peri|ít con|grés sus i|níquo`

### D. Caesura dash in a bad place (17)
- **Dash right after a proclitic** (*et, atque, ad, cum*), moved to the next legitimate caesura. That is the hephthemimeral, or the trochaic caesura where there is one. Lines: 87, 88, 90, 99, 188, 192, 213, 228, 237, 421, 530.
  - Example: *Experiere iterum et dominam — per verbera nosces*.
- **Same problem, but no other caesura available**, so the dash is dropped: 95 (*amiss'et — Trinacria*), 343 (*decus et — flammantia*), 356 (*aut — quosdam*; the comma before *aut* already gives the pause).
- **Punctuation already stands at the penthemimeral**, but the scanner missed it because of the prodelision *-'st*. The extra dash is removed: 487 (*caelum est, quae nos — huc*), 506 (*Haec acies — regum est,*).
- **174**: the dash fell between *perrarum* and *munus*, inside the parenthesis marked with `-`. Removed; the parenthesis dashes already give the pauses.

## Uncertain or left as scanned
- **372** *iubet habitare*: the transmitted text has four shorts in a row, so it does not scan. The scanner treats *habi-* as a spondee. A word may be missing (for example *hic*). Left as it is.
- **375** *flamma non ensis*: this needs *nōn* to be short. It was probably meant as *nec ensis*. Left as scanned; the accents read naturally.
- **Accepted as licences**:
  - 2 and 184: Ĭtalis.
  - 20 *decŭs*, 215 *gemitŭs*, 483 *colĕ*: lengthened at the caesura.
  - 333: *ultiŏ*.
  - 524: *bīfida*.
  - 96: synizesis in *Hispania*; 499: synizesis in *praemio*.
  - 101 and 339: *volvitur*.
- **94** *iniuria*: it could be 5 syllables or *in-jūria*. The accents are the same either way, so I left it.
- **91, 495**: elisions block the main caesuras, so only the trihemimeral is left. The dash after *Permixtae* / *Sustulerit* stays.
- **283, 457, 485**: the rule order keeps the unpunctuated penthemimeral dash, although the punctuated hephthemimeral would read a little better.
- **Note for other books** (scanner bugs behind groups B and D):
  - `NO_INIT_J` contains `io` and `iac`. This turns *Iovem, iacio, iaceo, iaculum* into vowel-initial words.
  - `V_STEMS` lacks `pulu`.
  - A word joined to *est* by prodelision is never treated as a caesura boundary.
